# RGP Theme 3 Project---Lucas-Carter-23636298
 
Controls:
WASD to move
SPACE to jump
Left Click to place fossils (selected in the UI) onto the Building Zone
Right Click to destroy/retrieve fossils on the Building Zone

You can wall jump and perform some extra tricks using it.
Go through the level and collect as many fossils as you can, there are 3 Skulls and 40 Bones to collect!.
Once you've collected as much as you want, return to base camp and build your display of skeletons!
Have Fun!
